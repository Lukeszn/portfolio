How a project runs

The same four steps whether you buy a package online or start with a quote.

  1. 1

    Discovery call

    We talk about your goals, your audience, your competitors, and what success looks like for you.

  2. 2

    Strategy and design

    I map out the structure and wireframes, then design the pages for you to review before anything is built.

  3. 3

    Build and optimise

    Clean code, fast load times, SEO foundations, and a layout that works properly on phones.

  4. 4

    Launch and support

    We go live together, then I stay on for 30 days to make sure everything performs as planned.
